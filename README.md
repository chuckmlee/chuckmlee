# Chuck Lee

Product leader in New York who builds AI systems that decide, act and prove they worked. Fifteen years building consumer products at Ground News, SmartNews, VICE, BuzzFeed, Fullscreen and Groupon.

**What I work on:** ranking and feeds, AI agents, and the evaluation that decides whether they ship (human annotation, LLM-as-judge, launch benchmarks).

- **Ground News** (Head of Product): made evaluation the launch gate for AI. Agentic discovery and ingestion grew to 22% of daily ingestion, and story clustering was measured against double-blind human annotation.
- **SmartNews** (Senior Director of Product): changed what the US For You feed optimized for, from short-term CTR to meaningful engagement, on the surface behind 90%+ of page views.
- **BuzzFeed** (Director of Product): built the first content ranking across articles, quizzes, BuzzFeed News and video, and owned search across brands.

I still prototype myself, mostly with Claude Code, Codex and MCP. This GitHub is where those prototypes, evals and PM tools live.
